# Coverage map — every section of Chapter 605, and what our forms do with it

Gated by `docs/coverage-605.py`. The event map runs from the world inward. This
runs from the statute inward, and catches the third thing neither of the others
can: a section of the Act **nobody ever considered**. An unconsidered provision
leaves no trace anywhere — not in the document, not in an event anyone thought
to list — so the only way to find it is to enumerate the chapter and answer for
each section in turn.

The section numbers and titles below are the complete list of 191 sections in
Chapter 605, read from the statutory text on Online Sunshine through the browser
on 15 August 2026. That is the denominator: 191 of 191 sections have a row, and
the checker fails if the table and the chapter ever disagree.

Dispositions:

  `non-variable`  the Act forbids varying it — s. 605.0105(3) for the general
                  act, s. 605.2107(1) for the protected series provisions. Our
                  forms conform; the cited sections are where they recite it.
  `varied`        our forms displace or supplement the statutory default
  `relied`        the default governs and we leave it deliberately
  `n/a`           nothing an operating agreement can address — department
                  filings, court procedure, foreign companies, or machinery for
                  transactions we do not offer

The `our sections` column lists section numbers as they appear in **at least
one** master; the forms number differently and the event map is what resolves a
provision per form. A note marked GAP is an absence nobody has ruled on, and the
checker lists every one on each run.
