revocation of voluntary dissolution. — If a series limited liability company that has been administratively dissolved is reinstated, or if a series limited liability company that voluntarily dissolved revokes its articles of dissolution before filing a statement of termination, both of the following apply: (1) Each protected series of the series limited liability company ceases winding up. (2) Section 605.0708 applies to the series limited liability company and to each protected series of the company, in accordance with s. 605.2108 . History. — s. 30, ch. 2025-162.
