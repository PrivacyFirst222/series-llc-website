This Privacy Policy explains how **FLORIDA PROTECTED SERIES, LLC - PS 1** and **FLORIDA PROTECTED SERIES, LLC - PS 2** (the same Company parties defined in our [Terms of Service](/terms); together, "we" and "us") collect, use, and disclose information when you use myfloridaseriesllc.com and our services (the "Services"). This Policy is part of, and incorporated into, our Terms of Service.

### 1. Our Approach

We collect the information we need to prepare and file your documents, provide registered agent service, and maintain your client portal — and not more. **We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.**

### 2. Information You Give Us

To prepare filings and provide the Services, we collect: your name and contact information (email, phone, mailing address); the LLC's proposed name and designators; principal office and mailing addresses; registered agent information; management structure; the names and addresses of members and managers; purpose and effective-date elections; protected series names; and the contents of your certifications and electronic signatures. If you use our registered agent service, we receive documents served on or mailed to your LLC at our address.

**Please note:** most of this information is filed with the Florida Division of Corporations and becomes **public record**. That is a function of state filing law, not our choice.

Our website forms do not request, and you should not enter into them, Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, government identification numbers, biometric data, or bank account numbers. If you purchase our EIN service, we collect the responsible party's Social Security number or ITIN separately, through a secure channel we designate, solely to prepare and submit IRS Form SS-4, and we do not retain it after the EIN is issued.

### 3. Payment Information

Payments are processed by **Square, Inc.** We never receive or store your full card number, CVV, or bank credentials. Square provides us a confirmation of payment and limited details (such as the last four digits and the name on the order). Square's handling of your information is governed by Square's own privacy policy.

### 4. Information Collected Automatically

Our host, **Vercel**, logs standard technical information: IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, and timestamps. We use a session cookie to keep you signed in to the client portal. The Site does not use third-party analytics, advertising pixels, or cross-site tracking.

### 5. Address Suggestions and Verification

When you type an address into our forms, that text is sent to **Smarty** (SmartyStreets, LLC), our address-data provider, to suggest matching addresses and to check the address against U.S. Postal Service records. This happens as you type and again when you continue past an address step. We use it only to help you enter an accurate address.

### 6. How We Use Information

To prepare and file your documents; to communicate with you about your order; to create and operate your client portal account; to provide registered agent service, including posting what we receive to your portal; to process payments and keep financial records; to prevent fraud and abuse; to comply with law; and to improve the Services.

We send service-related emails (order confirmations, portal invitations, password resets, notices that a document or legal mail is available). These are not marketing and cannot be opted out of while you have an active order or account.

### 7. When We Disclose Information

- **The Florida Division of Corporations**, to make your filings — this information becomes public record.
- **Service providers** who process information on our behalf and only for us: Square (payments), Neon (database), Vercel (hosting and document storage), Resend (email delivery), and Smarty (address data).
- **Legal process**: when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.
- **A successor** in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

We do not disclose your information to data brokers, lead generators, marketing firms, or affiliates for their own marketing.

### 8. Client Portal and Documents

Documents we prepare for you, and legal mail we receive as your registered agent, are stored in access-controlled storage and available for download when you sign in. The portal is download-only — you cannot upload documents to it, and we do not accept documents for storage. Retain your own copies.

### 9. Retention

We keep order and filing records for as long as needed to provide the Services and thereafter as long as reasonably necessary for legal, tax, and recordkeeping purposes — generally at least seven (7) years. Registered agent records are kept for the period Florida law requires. You may ask us to delete information that we are not required to keep.

### 10. Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit, hashed passwords, and vendors that maintain their own security programs. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Protect your portal password and tell us immediately if you believe your account has been compromised.

### 11. Your Choices and Rights

You may review and update your contact information, request a copy of the information we hold about you, request correction of inaccuracies, or request deletion of information we are not legally or operationally required to keep. To make a request, email support@myfloridaseriesllc.com. We will verify your identity before acting and will respond within 45 days (with one 45-day extension if reasonably needed, and notice to you).

**State privacy laws.** Residents of states with comprehensive consumer privacy laws may have additional rights, including rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a portable copy, and opt out of sale or targeted advertising. **We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising**, so there is nothing to opt out of; we honor the other rights as described above for all customers regardless of residence.

### 12. Children

The Services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we will delete it.

### 13. United States Only

We provide services relating to Florida entities and intend the Services for users in the United States. We do not target the Services to residents of the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom.

### 14. Changes

We may update this Policy. We will post the updated version with a new "Last updated" date, and for material changes we will notify account holders by email.
